## 🎬 Demo Flow (Step-by-Step)

1. **Start Recording**
   - Show landing page
   - Narrate introduction

2. **Login**
   - Enter credentials
   - Click "Sign In"
   - Wait for dashboard

3. **Create Project**
   - Click "New Project"
   - Enter topic: "The Future of AI in Healthcare"
   - Select: PowerPoint (.pptx)
   - Click "Initialize AI Engine"

4. **Outline Step**
   - Wait for outline generation
   - Point out 3D visualization
   - Add a section (demonstrate interactivity)
   - Remove a section (show real-time update)
   - Click "Generate Full Text"

5. **Content Generation**
   - Show loading state
   - Wait for completion
   - Transition to editor

6. **Editor**
   - Scroll through sections
   - Edit one section (show inline editing)
   - Mention auto-save

7. **Export**
   - Click "Export" button
   - Show download
   - Mention file format

8. **Closing**
   - Show project in sidebar
   - Summarize features
   - End recording

## 💬 Natural Transitions

- "Now let's..." → Action
- "Notice how..." → Highlight feature
- "This is interesting because..." → Explain benefit
- "You can also..." → Show alternative
- "Let me demonstrate..." → Show feature

---

## 🎨 Visual Best Practices

- **Slow, deliberate mouse movements**
- **Hover over buttons** before clicking
- **Pause 2-3 seconds** after each action
- **Zoom in** when showing text content
- **Highlight** the 3D visualization prominently
- **Smooth transitions** between sections

---

## 🚨 Common Issues to Avoid

- ❌ Rushing through steps
- ❌ Not showing the 3D visualization
- ❌ Skipping the editing demonstration
- ❌ Forgetting to show export
- ❌ Console errors visible
- ❌ Network timeouts not handled
